### Amun Thorns swept-arc fix
- **Root bug:** orbit swords tick-snapshot OBB at 200ms ticks missed enemies — swords traveled 112-224px tangentially between ticks but hitbox was only 20px wide
- **Fix:** swept-arc detection. Tick reduced to 50ms. For each enemy in radial band (`orbitRadius ± 36`), check if its angle (mod sector) falls within the arc swept since last tick. Damage per pass = `p.damage × (0.4 + thornsDmgBonus)`, scales to ~4.5 dmg/sec at lvl 3
- Removed `swordHalfWid`/`swordDps`, added sector-clamped sweep so adjacent swords cover overflow without double-hits
